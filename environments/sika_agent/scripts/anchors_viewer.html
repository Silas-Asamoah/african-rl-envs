    Rings follow Thought Anchors (Bogdan, Macar, Nanda and Conmy, 2025): each agent step is resampled and scored on the final wallet; importance is how far the outcome moves. User messages and tool results are hollow, sized by how much deleting them changes the agent's later steps. The neuron map follows neuron-explorer: one point per MLP neuron, layers as sheets, wiring from direct-path weights, lenses from the unembedding and from steering.
