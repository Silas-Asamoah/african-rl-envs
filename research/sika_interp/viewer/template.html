Twi and Twi–English text on this page (requests, model replies, reasoning) is model-generated or synthetic and has not yet been checked by a native speaker.

Methods: own copies of both models served with vLLM on a Prime Intellect H200; activations recorded on Modal. Probes are logistic regressions with folds split by seed, so twins never cross between training and test. Steering pushes a neuron's write direction at ±0.8 of the residual norm at one position.
