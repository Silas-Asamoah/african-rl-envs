Read-outs

Steering at every decision

Each cell: one neuron's write direction added at the positions where the model chooses its move (± dose × residual norm), at one decision. Colour: shift of the margin toward the right move (lime) or the wrong/harmful one (coral), in nats; or its percentile among 32 random directions at the same layer, decision, sign and dose.

Decision circuit

Neurons with the largest steering effect at one decision, by layer. Edge to the top: measured margin shift at this decision (lime toward the right move, coral away). Edges between neurons: direct-path weight (write · read, from the weights; not specific to this input). Hover to trace, click to open, ⌘/ctrl-click to pin.

Thought anchors

Filled ring: model turn, size = change in mean outcome replaying from it vs from the next turn (10 replays per turn); colour = what it did. Hollow ring: input, size = largest KL its removal causes in a later turn. Arcs: step → later turn, width = mean KL over that turn's tokens. Sentence shading: receiver-head score.

Select a step.

Receiver heads

Kurtosis of each full-attention head's vertical scores (mean over turns). Outlined: top 20.

Own copies of both models: vLLM 0.32.0 on Prime Intellect H200 for episodes; activations, steering and attention on Modal. sika-agent 0.3.1, matched tasks. Probes: logistic regression, folds split by seed. Steering: batched pushes checked against plain forward passes; shifts measured from a zero push through the same route. Neuron-explorer and Thought Anchors methods rebuilt for agents; neuron dashboards and circuit layout after Neuronpedia. Code: github.com/Silas-Asamoah/african-rl-envs.
